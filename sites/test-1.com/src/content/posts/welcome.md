Welcome! This is the first post on **Test-1**. It's a plain markdown file living at
`src/content/posts/welcome.md`, and everything you see here is rendered at build time
with zero client-side JavaScript.

## Editing posts

Open [/admin/](/admin/) to manage posts in Sveltia CMS. Create or edit an entry, and
the change is written straight back to `src/content/posts/` as markdown — Astro picks
it up on the next build.

## What you can write

Standard markdown works out of the box:

- **Bold** and _italic_ text
- Lists, like this one
- [Links](/about/) to other pages
- Code, images, and blockquotes
